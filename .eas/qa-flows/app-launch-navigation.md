# App launch and design

Preconditions: approved test environment/build, human-authorized run, isolated synthetic identities; read docs/qa/EXPO_QA_SETUP.md.

## Steps and acceptance
Launch approved build, confirm simulation disclosure, check safe area and no crash. With designated test session navigate Home, Accounts, Send, Activity, Profile; receipt is nested, not tab. Check Inter, semantic colors, contrast, visible labels, large text and small screen. Verify gallery is unavailable in release. Mark fixture-only evidence. No unauthorized route access.

## Report
PASS only with observed evidence; FAIL with reproducible defect; BLOCKED if prerequisite or test mechanism missing. Record platform, build SHA, contract SHA, sanitized evidence and limitations. Do not edit code, expose secrets, or claim unrun verification.
